Caption Vote Logger

A Firefox add-on that keeps a list of the captions you vote on in the New Yorker and CartoonStock caption contests, along with your votes, and lets you download the list as a CSV or text file.

Install in Firefox (v1.0.0)

Open this page in Firefox (version 115 or later), click the button, then click Add when Firefox asks. Updates install automatically.

How to use it

  1. Open the contest's voting page. If the add-on's popup shows an "Allow access" button, click it and reload the page. This may be needed once per site.
  2. Vote as usual. The number on the toolbar icon counts your votes. Your list is kept after you close Firefox, so you can vote over several days.
  3. Click the toolbar icon to see your totals and the last caption recorded.
  4. Click Download CSV or Download text. The file goes to your Downloads folder.
  5. To erase the list, click Clear, then click it again to confirm.

Good to know

  • Captions are recorded only when you click a vote button. The add-on reads the page and never clicks or votes for you.
  • Uninstalling the add-on deletes your list, so download first.
  • CartoonStock support was tested on a mock-up of its voting page and will be confirmed when voting opens on October 13.

Privacy

The add-on collects no personal information and sends nothing anywhere. Your votes are stored in your browser and leave it only when you download them. It can access only newyorker.com and cartoonstock.com.
